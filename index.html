About Me

Marketing decisions backed by better data.

I'm Sojib Mondol, a digital marketing specialist focused on paid advertising, analytics, and conversion measurement. I work with tools such as Meta Ads, Google Ads, Google Analytics 4, and Google Tag Manager.

My approach is practical: understand the business goal, set up the right measurement, check that events are working, and use the data to guide the next step.

I also build and test tracking setups on WordPress and e-commerce platforms such as Shopify and WooCommerce.
